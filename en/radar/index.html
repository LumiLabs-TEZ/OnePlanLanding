    Quick questions

    Does Radar sell tickets?

    No. Radar only watches prices and pings you with a direct booking link on the ticketing platform. Your money never passes through Radar.

    Really free?

    Really. Up to 5 active alerts at once, no fees, no account.

    Why the phone number?

    To ping you on Zalo when prices hit your target, and only for that. No spam, at most 2 messages a day per alert, cancel and they stop.

    Are alerts accurate?

    Before pinging you, Radar re-checks the price in real time. If it drifts more than 10% from the published fare, the alert is not sent.

    Does Radar watch hotel prices?

    Not yet. The Hotels tab compares room prices from Agoda, Booking.com and 50+ sites in one tap, synced with the route you are viewing. Zalo alerts for hotels are in the works.
